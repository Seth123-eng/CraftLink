Getting Started

New to CraftLink? Here's how to get started with our platform:

  • Create an account – Sign up as a client or craftsperson
  • Complete your profile – Add your skills, experience, and location
  • Post or find jobs – Clients post jobs, craftspeople apply
  • Connect & collaborate – Use our chat feature to discuss details
Tip: Complete your profile to increase your visibility!

For Clients

Post jobs and find the right craftsperson for your project:

  • Post a job – Describe your project and requirements
  • Review applicants – Browse through craftsperson profiles
  • Communicate – Chat with potential hires to discuss details
  • Manage jobs – Track your job postings and remove them when done
Tip: Be specific in your job description to attract the right candidates!

For Craftspeople

Find jobs and grow your business:

  • Browse jobs – Explore available job postings
  • Connect with clients – Chat with clients who posted jobs
  • Build your reputation – Deliver quality work and get reviews
  • Manage applications – Keep track of jobs you're interested in
Tip: Respond quickly to client messages to stand out!

Chat & Communication

Stay connected with clients and craftspeople:

  • Start a chat – Click the chat button on any job posting
  • Discuss details – Ask questions, share information, and negotiate
  • Private conversations – All chats are private and secure
  • Stay organized – Keep all your conversations in one place
Tip: Be professional and responsive in all communications!

Account & Security

Keep your account safe and secure:

  • Use a strong password – Combine letters, numbers, and symbols
  • Keep your profile updated – Maintain accurate information
  • Report issues – Contact us if you encounter any problems
  • Privacy matters – We protect your personal information
Tip: Always log out when using a shared device!

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click on the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. Choose whether you want to register as a client or a craftsperson, fill in your details, and follow the verification steps.

How do I post a job?

If you're registered as a client, navigate to the Job Postings page and use the "Post a New Job" section. Fill in the category and description, then click "Post Job".

How do I find jobs?

If you're registered as a craftsperson, browse the Job Postings page. Use the search bar and category filter to find jobs that match your skills and interests.

How do I chat with someone?

On any job posting, click the "Chat" button. This will open a private conversation with the job poster where you can discuss details, ask questions, and negotiate.

How do I remove my job posting?

If you're the job owner, you'll see a "Remove" button on your job card. Click it and confirm to remove the posting. You can also manage your jobs from the "My Job Postings" modal.

Is CraftLink free to use?

Yes! CraftLink is completely free for both clients and craftspeople. We believe in connecting people without barriers.

How do I update my profile?

Visit your profile page by clicking on your avatar or name. From there, you can update your personal information, skills, and preferences.

What should I do if I encounter an issue?

Use the "Contact Us" button at the top of this page to send us a message. Our support team will get back to you as soon as possible.
